Launcher 1.8.1 makes game updates easier to find while letting you keep playing your installed version.

## What changed

### Update from Home, or keep playing your current version
**Before:** After the launcher found a newer game release, Home could still show
Play as its main action, making the update easy to miss.
**Now:** Home shows **Update now** when a newer compatible release is available
on your installed game’s selected channel. If your current installation is ready
to play, **Play current version** lets you launch it without installing the update.
Playing does not change your channel or hide the update on future launches.
**How to use it:** Open the launcher and let its update check finish. Choose
**Update now** to install the offered game release, or **Play current version**
to keep using the installed build. Downloads still require your choice.

### Keep update choices accurate while checking or playing
**Before:** On Mac and Linux, a previous update offer could remain while a new
check was running, or an update found during gameplay could stay unavailable
after the game closed.
**Now:** The launcher clears stale offers during checks and restores the correct
update action after gameplay. Downloads keep their Pause or Cancel action, and
Playing stays disabled while the game is running.

## Known issues and compatibility

Available for Windows x64, Apple Silicon Mac and Linux x64/ARM64. This is a
launcher-only update; the current Stable game remains 0.7.2. Existing game data,
saves, settings and optional texture packs are preserved. Multiplayer players
still need matching game builds and supported US Xbox game data.

The ARM64 Linux launcher does not include a prebuilt ARM64 game; that game remains
a developer source build. Physical Steam Deck acceptance remains pending. The
Mac app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized; existing macOS installation guidance applies.
